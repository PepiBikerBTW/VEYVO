# Google Play closed test: tester invitation (cs + en)

Google requires a new personal developer account to run a **closed test with at least 12 testers who stay opted in for 14 consecutive days** before it may apply for production. Invite 15–20 people so a few drop-outs do not reset the plan. Never use paid tester services.

Setup before sending (Play Console):
1. Create a Google Group (groups.google.com), for example *veyvo-testeri*, with "Anyone on the web can ask to join" or invite-only, and paste its address into the closed track: *Testing › Closed testing › (track) › Testers › Google Groups*. (Alternative: an email list in Play Console; then skip step 1 of the invitation.)
2. Publish the closed release and wait until it is reviewed. Only then do the links below work.
3. Replace `{{GOOGLE_GROUP_URL}}` with the group's link (e.g. `https://groups.google.com/g/veyvo-testeri`). The other two links are fixed for the package `com.veyvo.coach`.
4. Send it from your own mailbox, so replies come to you, and note the date: day 1 of the 14 is the day the 12th tester has opted in.

Requirements for testers: an Android phone with Android 7.0 or newer and Google Play, and a Google account (Gmail). The same Google account must be used for the group, the opt-in and the Play Store on the phone.

dělám běžeckou aplikaci **VEYVO**: běžecký deník a plán tréninku. Zapíšeš běh a VEYVO spočítá tempo, ukáže, kolik ti chybí do cíle, navrhne další trénink a pošle ho do hodinek. Je zdarma, bez účtu a bez reklam a data zůstávají v telefonu.

Než ji Google pustí do obchodu Google Play, musí ji **aspoň 12 lidí testovat 14 dní v kuse**. Budu moc rád, když mezi nimi budeš.

**Co je potřeba (5 minut):**
1. Přidej se do skupiny testerů: {{GOOGLE_GROUP_URL}} (tlačítko *Připojit se ke skupině*).
2. Na stejném účtu Google otevři https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.veyvo.coach a klepni na **Stát se testerem**.
3. Nainstaluj VEYVO z Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.veyvo.coach (odkaz funguje až po kroku 2, někdy to trvá pár minut).

VEYVO není zdravotnický prostředek; rady k tréninku a jídlu jsou obecné.

Díky moc!

## English

**Subject:** Could you help me test my running app VEYVO? (14 days, Android)

Hi,

I am building **VEYVO**, a running journal and training plan. Log a run and VEYVO works out your pace, shows how far you are from your goal, suggests the next session and sends it to your watch. It is free, with no account and no ads, and your data stays on your phone.

Before Google lets it into the Google Play store, **at least 12 people have to test it for 14 days in a row**. I would be very grateful if you were one of them.

**What to do (5 minutes):**
1. Join the testers' group: {{GOOGLE_GROUP_URL}} (*Join group*).
2. With the same Google account, open https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.veyvo.coach and tap **Become a tester**.
3. Install VEYVO from Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.veyvo.coach (this link works only after step 2; sometimes it takes a few minutes).
4. **Keep the app installed for at least 14 days and do not leave the test.** Otherwise Google's day count stops.

**What to try during those 14 days (a few times is enough):**
- log a few runs (for example 5 km in 27:30) and look at Overview, Plan and Progress;
- fill in recovery (sleep, fatigue, muscle soreness) with three taps on Overview;
- in Plan, add a session from the library and use **Send to watch** to get it onto your watch, or as a file or text;
- open **Before the run** and go through the timeline and the checklist;
- in Settings, switch the language and the theme, and save a backup to a file.

**Feedback:** reply to this email, or send private feedback on the VEYVO page in Google Play. It helps to add your phone model and what you were doing. Every note counts, even "this makes no sense to me".

The app asks for no account and no payment and sends nothing to me: your runs stay on your phone. Privacy policy: https://pepibikerbtw.github.io/VEYVO/privacy/

VEYVO is not a medical device; its training and food tips are general guidance.

Thank you!
Pepa (Josef Kožíšek)
